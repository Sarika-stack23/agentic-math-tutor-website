Agentic Math Tutor

Welcome to the Agentic Math Tutor, an AI-powered, step-by-step mathematical tutoring application built exclusively for the Anna App Runtime.

What it does

Our application allows students to input math problems (or upload images of math problems) and receive step-by-step, teacher-style guidance. Instead of just giving you the answer, we help you understand the core concepts.

Core Features

  • Pedagogical Focus: We act as a tutor, not an answer key.
  • Step-by-step Guidance: Breaks down complex problems into digestible steps.
  • Hints & Explanations: Ask for hints, deeper explanations, or the reasoning behind a step.
  • Practice: Generate similar practice problems.
  • Check Your Work: Submit your own attempt and get learning-focused feedback.
  • Secure Runtime: Powered by the Anna Host environment with a secure, sandboxed execution engine for mathematical computations.
