Support - Agentic Math Tutor

If you are experiencing issues with the Agentic Math Tutor, please review the information below.

How to use the app

The Agentic Math Tutor operates entirely within the Anna App Runtime.

  1. Enter your mathematical problem into the chat interface.
  2. Use the action buttons (Hint, Steps, Answer, Why?, Teach Me) to guide your learning experience.
  3. If you want to verify your own understanding, use the "Check My Work" feature to input your attempt.

Known Limitations

  • The Python numerical execution sandbox is designed for educational math workflows and restricts network/filesystem access.
  • Image parsing capabilities depend on the vision model provided by your Anna Host.
