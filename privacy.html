Privacy Policy - Agentic Math Tutor

Last Updated: September 2026

The Agentic Math Tutor is designed to run locally within your Anna Host environment. We are committed to your privacy and transparent data practices.

Data Collection & Storage

  • Application Storage: All conversational history and mathematical state are stored locally on your device via the anna.storage API. We do not transmit your history to a centralized cloud database.
  • LLM Processing: Mathematical queries are processed by the Language Model provided by your Anna Host (anna.llm.stream). No API keys or external developer accounts are bundled.
  • Uploaded Images: Images uploaded for mathematical OCR are encoded locally and processed by the Anna Host.
  • Math Tool Execution: Numerical and symbolic computations are executed securely within local sandboxed Executas (math-sympy, math-pyexec, math-graph).

Third-Party Services

This application does not bundle external trackers, analytics, or third-party databases (Firebase is not used in the production Anna runtime). All external calls are mediated strictly through the Anna Host APIs.

Data Retention

Because data is stored via anna.storage using a last_writer_wins merge strategy, your data remains in your local Anna environment and is retained according to your Host's storage policies.
